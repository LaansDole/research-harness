A researcher picks up her scoping review

She is reviewing how teams of LLM agents support clinical decisions. One command, research web, opens the agent in her browser on her own machine (127.0.0.1), beside her projects. In the take she makes the review the agent's project and asks which paper to read first, reopens an earlier session, triages the reading list, reads the review and picks out a claim to check, explores the paper graph and the PRISMA diagram, recovers from a stopped agent, and finishes on a phone-width screen.

Chapters 18

How it was recorded

6 min 0 s, recorded live from headless Chrome with Puppeteer's page.screencast() (script) at 1440x900 and shown at 1x. The phone-width part is a second recording at 430x900, letterboxed and joined after the first; that join is the only cut. research web and its agent ran with a scratch home folder holding a copy of her project, so only sessions made there appear and nothing in the real research folder changed.

What the agent really did

The agent's answers are real (claude-opus-5-5, thinking medium): three prompts are sent during the take (the project switch, Which paper should I read first? and a summary request she stops with Esc), and the earlier session she reopens is a real turn sent while preparing the scratch folder. A screencast can't follow a new tab, so Open PDF is shown by loading the PDF in the same tab and coming back.

What's on screen

Every part of research web the take passes through.

Header and tabs
The header shows the project she is viewing, whether the agent works on it (an Agent project tag, or a Make active button when it doesn't), the agent's status (Ready, Working…, Connecting…, Disconnected) and a sun/moon button for dark mode. Arrow keys, Home and End move between the Chat, Graph, Reviews, Reading list and PRISMA tabs; the sidebar button hides the sidebar for more room and Ctrl/⌘+B brings it back.
Chat
Make active writes the switch into the message box; she sends it with Enter, the status turns Working…, the agent's steps appear while it works, and the answer brings a table of where the review stands. Meanwhile she types Which paper should I read first?, which waits its turn and is answered next. A fork tried mid-answer gets a short notice that it couldn't be done, which she dismisses. Each answer keeps its thinking and tool steps folded under thinking · N steps, so the answer stays in front, and once the agent is done the header and sidebar show her review as the agent's project.
Message box
She attaches an image and removes it, the model picker shows claude-opus-5-5, and she changes the thinking level from medium to high and back. A two-line question (Shift+Enter starts the second line) goes out with the Send button; Esc stops it, and the reply reads Stopped before finishing. In a new session she types a plan for the day, a starter command replaces it, and Restore previous draft brings it back.
Sidebar and sessions
Projects with screening progress (9 of 12 screened, No records yet) and an agent tag on the agent's project; sessions from all projects, newest first. She reopens an earlier session and unfolds its steps: a thinking note, and a tool card whose long output expands and collapses. Fork copies that session and New session starts an empty one, whose starter commands fit her review.
Reading list
The next-step bar (1 full text ready · Screen full texts) writes that command into the message box. State chips with counts filter the list, by click or arrow keys; Excluded · T/A lists the five title/abstract exclusions, and a record's panel shows its verdict and the reason recorded. ↑/↓, Enter, Esc and the Previous/Next buttons walk the records, and Ask agent to reconsider writes a request that quotes the recorded reason. Searching triage finds TriageAgent, with both screening verdicts, its DOI and Open PDF, which shows the paper; a search with no matches says No records match this filter.
Reviews
The newest review file opens first, with a table and a formula; the file picker switches to her mini-review. Contents jumps to a section, a numbered citation jumps to its reference (DOI linked) and Back returns. She selects one sentence and Check claim writes a request naming the file, the project and the sentence; each section also has Revise and Check, which write their own requests.
Paper graph
Hovering a paper labels it and its neighbours. Clicking TriageAgent opens its details and three actions, Find citing papers, Auto-edges and Explain neighbors, each writing a request into the message box. She closes the details, drags a paper, pans and zooms, checks the legend of link types, searches galatea (Enter picks the most-connected match) and clears the selection. The sun/moon button turns the whole app dark, the graph included, and back to light.
PRISMA
The PRISMA-ScR flow diagram, every count taken from her records, with a Download SVG button.
An empty project
Viewing an empty project, the header offers Make active instead of the tag, and PRISMA, the reading list and the reviews each say what to do next, with a button that writes it into the message box, naming the project.
Connection
An old link to a project name that no longer exists still opens her project's reviews. When the agent process stops, a banner says so and Reconnect starts a fresh agent; her sessions are still there, and one reopens with its whole conversation.
Phone width
At 430 px the sidebar becomes a drawer that Esc or a tap outside closes, a record opens as a full-screen sheet with Next, the review gets a Contents menu, and PRISMA gets a Fit / 100 % switch; at 100 % the diagram scrolls sideways.

The exported paper graph

The Graph tab shows the paper graph's own export: one self-contained HTML file that draws the papers as a network you can drag, zoom, pan and search. Bigger dots have more links, line colours show the link type, and clicking a paper shows its details. It loads nothing from the internet, so it also opens straight from disk.

How it fits together

The browser and the terminal talk to the same research agent. research web only reads her projects; the agent's commands run small Python scripts, which keep the screening records and the paper graph and fetch open-access PDFs. The architecture guide walks through every layer.
